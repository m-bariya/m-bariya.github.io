General

Beyond my academic publications, I periodically write on technical topics for a broader audience. These writings are collected here.

nLine blog

While at nLine, I co-wrote several blog posts describing our technical work and application areas. These are helpful to understand how edge sensing can enable improved distribution system visibility and the widespread service quality challenges in global south grids.

  • Helping Global Grids Work Smarter, not Harder
  • What Can Voltages Tell Us About the Structure of the Grid?
  • From Measurements to KPIs: Estimating SAIDI at nLine
  • A Clustering Algorithm for Power Outage Detection

A National Infrastructure for AI on the Grid

I wrote a series of technical blogs for the ARPA-E funded ni4ai initiative, which aimed to provide an engaging and accessible introduction to various topics in power systems and grid measurement analysis, particularly focused on applications of high-resolution, time-synchronized data.

  • An introduction to calculating power factors from phasor data
  • An introduction to symmetrical components
  • How should we understand voltage angles in a network
  • Data-driven solar disaggregation
