#### A National Infrastructure for AI on the Grid
I wrote a series of technical blogs for the ARPA-E funded [ni4ai initiative](https://energycentral.com/c/iu/arpa-e-funded-platform-ni4ai-democratizing-grid-data), which aimed to provide an engaging and accessible introduction to various topics in power systems and grid measurement analysis, particularly focused on applications of high-resolution, time-synchronized data.
- [An introduction to calculating power factors from phasor data](https://blog.ni4ai.org/post/2021-06-19-power_factor/)
- [An introduction to symmetrical components](https://blog.ni4ai.org/post/2020-11-19-symmetrical-components/)
- [How should we understand voltage angles in a network](https://blog.ni4ai.org/post/2021-04-25-angle-differencing/)
- [Data-driven solar disaggregation](https://blog.ni4ai.org/post/2021-05-29-disaggregation/)
